First-time setup

A small wallet, a clear next step.

You can browse every public purchase without a wallet. Connect Meteor only when you want HarvestPool to check eligibility or ask you to approve a transaction.

Never share a seed phrase or private key with HarvestPool.
01 · What it is

Your wallet is your account and signature.

It holds your NEAR account access and asks you to approve actions. HarvestPool never asks for or stores your seed phrase, private key, or funds.

02 · Why you need it here

Browsing stays public. Actions need proof.

Your wallet connects your account and signs contributions, decisions, fulfillment approval, refunds, and other purchase actions.

03 · Connect Meteor

Use the same account you want to test.

  1. Install or open Meteor Wallet from its official site.
  2. Make sure the wallet has a NEAR Testnet account selected; test accounts commonly end in .testnet.
  3. Return here and choose Connect wallet. Approve the connection in Meteor.
  4. Check that the account shown in the wallet panel is the account you intended to use.
  5. If you use the extension, allow it to access 127.0.0.1:5010 in the browser’s extension settings. If you use the web wallet, allow its popup or redirect.
Open Meteor Wallet ↗
04 · Get free testnet NEAR

Use a faucet, never real funds.

Use a Testnet faucet for the account shown in Meteor, then wait for the balance to appear. Testnet NEAR has no cash value.

Open testnet faucet ↗

If that faucet is unavailable, use a current faucet linked from NEAR’s testnet documentation. Do not send mainnet NEAR to a testnet account.

05 · Approve safely

Read the wallet prompt before every action.

Join & Contribute

Check the purchase ID, quantity, exact USD estimate, exact NEAR amount, and contract account before approving the financial commitment.

Cast Your Vote

Voting decisions attach 0 NEAR. Only funded members can vote on fulfillment; network gas may still apply.

After signing

Wait for HarvestPool to reread the contract. A button click or wallet window alone is never shown as success.
